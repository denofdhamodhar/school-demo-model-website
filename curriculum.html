Curriculum Stages (K–12 Hierarchy)

Sequential developmental stages supporting academic and personal growth.

STAGE 1Early Years (Nursery – Kindergarten)

Ages 3 to 5

Play-based learning, foundational literacy, numeracy, motor skill development, and social bonding.

STAGE 2Primary School (Grades 1 – 5)

Grades I to V

Core academic concepts, language proficiency, activity-based science, and artistic expression.

STAGE 3Middle School (Grades 6 – 8)

Grades VI to VIII

Analytical thinking, structured scientific inquiry, digital literacy, and collaborative projects.

STAGE 4Secondary School (Grades 9 – 12)

Grades IX to XII

Rigorous board preparation, specialized stream electives (Science, Commerce, Humanities), and career counseling.

[CONTINUOUS EVALUATION]

Assessment & Evaluation Philosophy

Evaluation at Demo Academy combines Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) with periodic formative and summative assessments. We place equal emphasis on practical lab sessions, oral presentations, collaborative group work, and written examinations.

Formative Assessment (40%): Quizzes, lab experiments, class discussions, and project portfolios.
Summative Assessment (60%): Mid-term and end-of-term board-aligned examinations.
Co-Curricular Grades: Physical education, arts, discipline, and community leadership ratings.
