School History & Educational Philosophy

Founded in 1995, DEMO ACADEMY SCHOOL was established with a singular focus: to make world-class holistic education accessible, structured, and empowering. Over the past three decades, the school has grown from a humble single building into a modern multi-acre educational hub.

We follow a balanced pedagogical model that fuses rigorous analytical science and mathematics with expressive humanities, fine arts, and competitive physical education. Our faculty members act as mentors, encouraging students to ask deep questions, conduct hands-on experiments, and build compassionate leadership habits.

Our Vision

To be a premier global center of learning that fosters intellectual curiosity, ethical character, and innovative problem-solving capability in every child, preparing them to thrive and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive, and technologically advanced learning environment where students master fundamental concepts, develop emotional resilience, celebrate diversity, and cultivate lifelong habits of inquiry.
