Required Documents

  • Student Original Birth Certificate & 2 Photocopies
  • Previous School Transfer Certificate (TC) signed by Board
  • Report Cards of previous 2 academic years
  • 4 passport-sized photographs of student & parents
  • Proof of residential address (Aadhaar / Passport / Utility bill)

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nursery: Minimum age 3 years as of March 31, 2026
  • Grade 1: Minimum age 6 years as of March 31, 2026
  • Secondary (Grades 9-11): Subject to qualifying entrance evaluation and past academic transcripts.
